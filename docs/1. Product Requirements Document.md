# Product Requirements Document: LifeOps

**Status:** v1.0 — Draft  
**Last Updated:** May 29, 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary

LifeOps is an AI-powered personal chief of staff that ingests data from every app a person uses, understands their patterns across all life domains simultaneously, reasons across that data, and takes real action — not just gives advice. It solves the fragmentation problem: the average person uses 12+ apps to manage their life, none of which share context with each other.

**Vision:** One intelligent agent that connects every app in your life, understands your patterns, reasons across every domain simultaneously, takes real action on your behalf, and gets measurably smarter about you every single week.

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Persona: The Overloaded Professional
- Age: 25–45
- Uses 10+ apps daily for work, finances, health, and social life
- Feels like life moves faster than they can manage it
- Has disposable income but limited time
- Values proactive intelligence over reactive tools

### Secondary Personas
- **The Budget-Conscious Organizer** — wants visibility into spending without manual tracking
- **The Health-Conscious Professional** — wants sleep, fitness, and stress patterns connected
- **The Social Connector** — wants to never miss a birthday or commitment

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Every day, a person's life generates data across dozens of apps — calendar, bank, fitness tracker, email, grocery, streaming, notes. None of these apps share context with each other. The mental cost of bridging these gaps manually:

- 2+ hours per day in cognitive overhead
- $840/year in forgotten subscriptions
- 3–4 missed social commitments per month
- Predictable stress spirals that go uncaught
- Decisions made without the complete picture

LifeOps exists to connect these dots automatically and proactively.

---

## 4. Product Principles

1. **Proactive by Default** — LifeOps acts without being asked. Morning briefs, pattern alerts, and opportunity detection run 24/7.
2. **Action-Oriented** — LifeOps does not just recommend; it executes (with user approval).
3. **Cross-Domain Reasoning** — No silos. A financial decision factors in health, schedule, and social context.
4. **Permanent Memory** — LifeOps remembers everything and gets richer over time.
5. **Measurable Improvement** — The system quantitatively tracks its own performance and self-improves weekly.
6. **User Control** — LifeOps never acts without explicit user approval. The user's judgment is always final.

## 5. Feature Requirements

### 5.1 Core Features (P0 — Launch Critical)

| Feature | Description | User Value |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Data Ingestion (Fivetran) | Auto-sync 12+ apps into central warehouse | Zero manual data entry |
| Morning Brief | Daily 7am AI-generated brief covering schedule, health, finances, social, and risks | Start every day with full context |
| Cross-Domain Q&A | Natural language queries that reason across all data simultaneously | Ask anything, get comprehensive answers |
| Proactive Pattern Alerts | Detect and notify about stress spirals, budget risks, health declines before they escalate | Prevent problems, not react to them |
| Action Execution | Execute user-approved actions: calendar blocks, grocery orders, subscription cancellations | From insight to action in one tap |
| Self-Improvement Loop | Weekly trace analysis via Arize Phoenix to improve recommendation quality | System gets smarter every week |

### 5.2 Enhanced Features (P1 — Post-Launch)

| Feature | Description | Priority Rationale |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Dinner Party Planner | Full event planning across guest dietary restrictions, budget, past parties, weather | Showcases cross-domain power |
| Social Commitment Tracking | Detect birthdays, anniversaries, promises from email | Expands relational intelligence |
| Financial Deep-Dive | Subscription audit, spending category analysis, savings optimization | High-value but complex |
| Weekly/Monthly Reports | Periodic summaries across all domains | Lower urgency than daily brief |

### 5.3 Future Features (P2 — Backlog)

- Multi-user household coordination
- Shared family budget and calendar

## 6. User Stories

### Morning Brief
> As a user, I want a daily morning summary that covers my schedule, sleep, budget status, and social commitments so I start my day with full context and zero manual check-in.

### Stress Pattern Interrupt
> As a user, I want LifeOps to detect when I'm entering a known stress spiral pattern and offer specific mitigations before it escalates.

### Cross-Domain Question
> As a user, I want to ask "How am I doing?" and get a synthesized answer covering my health, finances, productivity, and social life — not just one dimension.

### Subscription Audit
> As a user, I want LifeOps to identify unused subscriptions and offer to cancel them with one tap.

### Event Planning
> As a user, I want LifeOps to plan a dinner party considering guest dietary restrictions, my budget, past successful menus, and weather conditions.

## 7. Success Metrics

### Engagement Metrics
- DAU/MAU ratio > 60%
- Morning Brief open rate > 85%
- Weekly active user growth > 10% MoM

### Effectiveness Metrics
- Average recommendation follow-through rate > 60% (target 75% by week 12)
- User-reported cognitive load reduction (survey): target 40% reduction
- Forgotten subscription savings per user: target $500+/year

### Technical Metrics
- Self-improvement score improvement: from 3.2/5 (week 1) to 4.7/5 (week 6)
- Query response time: < 2 seconds for cross-domain queries
- Data sync latency: < 15 minutes for critical apps
- System uptime: 99.9%

---

## 8. Assumptions & Constraints

### Assumptions
- Users have accounts on 5+ of the supported 12+ apps
- Users grant OAuth-level API access to their data
- Users are willing to receive proactive notifications daily
- Users will trust AI to execute actions after approval
- Users have stable internet connectivity

### Constraints
- Must comply with GDPR, CCPA, and data privacy regulations
- Must never expose raw personal data to unauthorized parties
- Must maintain user control — never auto-execute without explicit approval
- Fivetran connector availability limits initial integration set
- Gemini 2.5 Pro API rate limits and latency

## 11. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| User data privacy concerns | High | Transparent data usage policy; on-device processing where possible; SOC 2 compliance |
| API deprecation from connected apps | Medium | Abstract connector layer; fallback to manual import |
| Low recommendation follow-through | High | Self-improvement loop specifically targets this; A/B test recommendation styles |
| User notification fatigue | Medium | Smart notification throttling; user-customizable frequency |
| LLM hallucination on personal data | High | Ground all outputs in retrieved data; show sources; user approval required for actions |
